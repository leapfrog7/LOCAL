WHAT IT HELPS YOU DO

From a paper page to something you can find and reuse.

LOCAL focuses on everyday document work. It keeps the original capture, prepares searchable text and gives you page-level tools without requiring an online account.

01
⌗

Scan and import

Capture multi-page paper documents, adjust page edges, improve readability, or import an existing PDF—including password-protected files.

02
⌕

Search recognised text

On-device OCR makes printed text searchable. Jump to matching pages, then organise documents with folders and tags.

03
Aa

Read and reuse text

Use continuous page scrolling, bookmarks and text view. Select several pages and copy or export their recognised text together.

04
✎

Annotate pages

Add pen and highlighter marks with undo and erasing. Choose whether to save a copy or update the document.

05
⇄

Prepare PDF copies

Combine documents, extract or reorder pages, insert pages, review cleanup suggestions and compress PDFs for sharing.

06
◇

Protect and recover

Use app lock, private documents, PDF export passwords and passphrase-encrypted backups for different protection needs.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

Your library is not a cloud account.

Document images, recognised text, metadata and search indexes are stored in LOCAL's private app storage. OCR and document search run on the device.

Read the privacy policy →
01
No analytics or advertising

LOCAL does not include usage tracking, ad software or an account system.

02
You initiate anything leaving the app

A document leaves LOCAL when you choose to export, share or save a backup. Android components required for on-device recognition may be downloaded separately.

03
Private and password-protected mean different things

Private documents require authentication inside LOCAL. A PDF password protects a PDF copy outside LOCAL.

GOOD TO KNOW

Clear expectations make a trustworthy tool.

Backups are your responsibility

LOCAL does not automatically sync your library. Create an encrypted backup and keep its passphrase separately.

OCR is an aid, not the original

Faint print, handwriting and complex layouts can be recognised incorrectly. Check important text against the page image.

PDF tools work with pages

LOCAL can arrange, annotate, extract and regenerate PDFs. It does not edit existing PDF text or preserve every interactive form, link or digital signature in regenerated copies.
