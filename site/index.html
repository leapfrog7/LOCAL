PRIVATE BY DESIGN

No cloud document pipeline hiding behind the interface.

LOCAL has no analytics, advertising, account system, remote OCR or cloud synchronization. Google Play services may download ML Kit components; document processing stays on the device. Files leave LOCAL only when you explicitly export, share or back them up.
