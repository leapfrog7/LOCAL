Privacy policy

Data collection

LOCAL has no account service and does not include analytics, advertising identifiers, usage telemetry or developer-operated crash reporting. The app does not send document contents or personal information to a developer-controlled server.

On-device data

Scans, imported pages, thumbnails, generated PDFs, recognised text, folders, tags and search indexes are stored in private app storage. Database records use SQLCipher. Documents marked Private also encrypt stored page and PDF files with an Android Keystore-backed key.

Permissions and network access

Camera access is used to scan. Storage access on older Android versions is used for exports initiated by the user. LOCAL does not upload documents for OCR, search or synchronization. Google Play services may download or update components needed for on-device ML Kit recognition; document processing remains on the device.

Sharing and backups

A document leaves LOCAL when you choose to export, share or open a file in another app, save an unlocked PDF copy, or save a backup outside LOCAL's private storage. Encrypted backups are protected by the passphrase you choose; LOCAL cannot recover a forgotten passphrase.

Retention and deletion

Documents moved to Recently Deleted can be restored for up to 30 days unless you permanently delete them sooner. Removing the application removes its private app data. Android automatic backup and device-transfer extraction are disabled for LOCAL.

Contact

Questions and issue reports can be raised through the GitHub issue tracker.
