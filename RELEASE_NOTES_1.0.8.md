# LOCAL 1.0.8

- Prevents backup restore data corruption from duplicate or cross-document page identifiers.
- Keeps private filenames, OCR text, tags, previews and search matches hidden until authentication.
- Safely removes obsolete plaintext IndexedDB records after verified migration to encrypted native storage.
- Adds coloured Scan, Import PDF and Tools actions with a clearer first-use Home experience.
- Replaces the short Settings guide with Discover LOCAL, covering scanning, search, reading, text extraction, page tools, privacy and encrypted backups.
- Simplifies Home to actions, one search field and Recent documents. Less-used filters, sorting, date range, layout and search tips now live behind one search-options button.
- Adds clearer saved/searchable status messages and Undo after moving documents to Recently Deleted.

LOCAL continues to process documents on the device. You choose what to export or share.
